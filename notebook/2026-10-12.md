## 1. Why the thermal YOLO "shoots too much" in the pipeline (run 2026-10-12T07_14_47, Arsuf bench GT)

Pipeline counts (Daniel): thermal 18,135 nvinfer boxes, all reaching the Detection Manager; day 5,276, of which 1,430 removed by the edge filter and 2,083 by the confidence filter.

**What the pipeline runs** (`eval/meta.json`, `configs/`):
- Thermal = **M0 s0** (`yolo26sp2_thermal_20261007`, classes 0 bird / 1 thermal-uav), exported at **640×640** (trained and validated at 512), `pre-cluster-threshold 0.25`, and **no edge or confidence filter stage** for thermal (day: pre-cluster 0.05, then edge + confidence filters).
- So "the thermal has no low-confidence boxes" is the 0.25 cut-off, not over-confidence: logged confidences run 0.25–0.92, median 0.54 (day median 0.14).

**Box quality against the GT** (pipeline frames matched to labels by time; same hit rule as score_models):

| | recall | empty frames with a box | off-target boxes / labelled frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| pipeline (M0 s0 engine, 640, ≥ 0.25) | 54.4 % | 49.2 % | 0.59 |
| M0 s0 offline @512, ≥ 0.25 | 53.4 % | 49.7 % | 0.38 |
| M0 s0 offline @640, ≥ 0.25 | 63.7 % | 65.9 % | 0.68 |

The engine scores like the model offline (no parsing bug); the export resolution adds off-target boxes. Offline at 640 is clearly worse at matched false fires than at 512.

**Threshold sweep (offline, empty-frame false fire / recall):**

| thr | M0 s0 @512 | M0 s0 @640 | M0 s1 @512 | S1 s0 @512 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.25 | 49.7 / 53.4 | 65.9 / 63.7 | **31.1 / 51.8** | 50.8 / 59.4 |
| 0.35 | 39.8 / 48.8 | 52.8 / 59.4 | 24.3 / 47.3 | 37.8 / 56.7 |
| 0.45 | 21.5 / 41.8 | 42.9 / 53.9 | 13.6 / 41.8 | 20.8 / 49.0 |
| 0.50 | 11.0 / 36.3 | 36.0 / 48.9 | 7.7 / 37.1 | 12.2 / 39.8 |
| 0.60 | 3.3 / 22.0 | 22.1 / 32.7 | 1.4 / 24.1 | 3.2 / 19.8 |

- **The deployed seed (M0 s0) is the worst-calibrated of the M family**: at 0.25 it fires on 50 % of empty frames; M0 s1 on 31 % with the same recall.
- The ranking is weak overall: halving false fires costs ~10 recall points for every model. That is the real problem the training must fix.

**What the false alarms are** (M0 s0 @512, ≥ 0.25: 1,222 off-target boxes over 3,186 labelled frames):
- Not label errors: 467 of 472 empty-frame false alarms have no labelled drone within 1 s and 100 px.
- Mostly **tiny bright dots, median 6 px**, in the sky and near the horizon (sensor speckle, distant birds or clutter); plus cloud edges, sun glare and frame-edge/tent hits; and 227 secondary boxes 30–100 px from the real drone (fragments).
- Some positions repeat for minutes, but they are near the frame centre where the gimbal keeps the target, not fixed sensor defects (no hot pixel in the temporal median there).

**Hypothesis (Daniel: "not enough hard negatives"): yes, and the pastes make it worse.**
- ~8 % of M0's training images are empty (742 of 8,799 in the boson3PR build; similar in bosonP).
- ~1.8k training images are synthetic pastes of small bright sprites on smooth sky. That teaches exactly "small bright dot on sky = drone": the synthA v1 lesson from 09-29, now on clean sky.

## 2. Plan (criteria written before the runs)

**A. Deployment, no training (to try on the Jetson now):**
1. Export the thermal model at **512** (its training size).
2. Use **M0 s1** instead of s0: same recall, about 40 % fewer false fires at every threshold.
3. Thermal threshold **0.45–0.5** instead of 0.25, or add a thermal confidence-filter stage like the day one. At 0.5, M0 s1: 7.7 % empty-frame false fire, 37 % recall (vs 31 % / 52 % at 0.25).

**B. Training runs** (when an L4 is available again; us-west1-a had none on 10-12 morning):

| run | change vs M0 data (uav = 0 copy) | question |
|---|---|---|
| **N1** | + real hard negatives: frames from TRAINING sessions only where the current models fire ≥ 0.35 away from the confirmed drone (the drone box stays in the frame), ×2; + confirmed-empty frames where they fire | do real hard negatives cut false fires? |
| **N2** | pastes halved (synthetic share ~13 % instead of ~26 %) | are the pastes the source of the dot false alarms? |
| **N3** | N1 + N2 | combined |

- Mining sources: 06-23 s1/s2/s3, 07-15 frames < 9,775, Boson 10-06 training sessions (status `pos` = drone confirmed by both cameras, `neg` = both silent). Never 07-02 (the Arsuf bench), 07-30, or the Boson held-out 08_58_15 / 10_15_59.
- 2 seeds each, recipe as M0 (512).
- **Primary metric: empty-frame false fire at the threshold that gives M0's recall at 0.25**, on the Arsuf GT (727 empty frames) and on boson_0858/1015, plus mean recall FF 1–25 %.
- **Adopt** a run if it lowers empty-frame false fire by ≥ 30 % relative (seed means, beyond the seed spread) on the Arsuf GT and on boson_1015, without a mean-recall drop beyond the seed spread on boson_0858/1015.
- Also log boxes per frame at 0.5, which is the pipeline's view.
