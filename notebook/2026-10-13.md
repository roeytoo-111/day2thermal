## 3. Weight averaging without training (CPU, today)

- **EMA is already on**: Ultralytics trains with an EMA of the weights (decay 0.9999, tau 2000) and saves the EMA copy as best.pt; every model scored so far is EMA.
- **Model soup of same-start runs (M0 s0 + M4 s0, identical initial weights, different data; relative weight distance 0.46): fails.** Raw average fires on 65 / 3,396 Arsuf frames even at 0.05; with batch-norm statistics re-estimated on 480 unlabelled training-session frames it works again but is worse than both members (Arsuf mean recall FF 1–25 %: 27.9 vs 35.0 / 32.2). Soups across seeds are ruled out a priori (different random heads).
- **BN re-estimation alone on M0 s0 (no averaging, "AdaBN" with new-camera training frames):** Arsuf 35.0 -> 38.8 (old camera), boson_1015 56.2 -> 54.6 (deployed camera). It mostly lowers all confidences (fewer false fires at a fixed threshold, lower recall); no gain at matched false fire on the camera that matters. Not useful for deployment.

## 4. Queue v10 (when an L4 is available): SWA and ensemble distillation; criteria fixed now

- **SWA** (`src/train/swa_average.py`): continue N2 s0 and M0 s1 for 15 epochs at constant lr 0.0005 from their best.pt, average the last 10 epoch checkpoints, re-estimate BN on 1,500 training images. **Kept** if the SWA model beats its own source model on mean recall FF 1–25 % on BOTH Arsuf and boson_1015 by > 2 pts, with boson_0858 not lower by > 1 frame (paired, same seed).
- **Ensemble distillation** (`src/data/make_pseudo_labels.py`): teacher = ensemble A (M0 s1 + N2 s0 + R0 s0), run on unlabelled frames of training sessions only (Boson training sessions, 06-23 s2 < 380 s, 07-15 < 370 s; held-out / 07-02 / 07-30 excluded); fused ≥ 0.5 -> uav box, all < 0.15 -> empty, in between -> skipped. Student **KD1** = N2 data + pseudo-labels, yolo26s-p2 from rgbcurrent, seeds 0 and 1. **Adopted** over N2 if its seed mean beats N2's on Arsuf and boson_1015 by more than the larger seed spread, with no 0858 drop beyond spread; the false-fire sweep is reported alongside. The ensemble A itself (Arsuf 46.8, 1015 62.1) is the ceiling to compare with.
