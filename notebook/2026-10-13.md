## 3. Weight averaging without training (CPU, today)

- **EMA is already on**: Ultralytics trains with an EMA of the weights (decay 0.9999, tau 2000) and saves the EMA copy as best.pt; every model scored so far is EMA.
- **Model soup of same-start runs (M0 s0 + M4 s0, identical initial weights, different data; relative weight distance 0.46): fails.** Raw average fires on 65 / 3,396 Arsuf frames even at 0.05; with batch-norm statistics re-estimated on 480 unlabelled training-session frames it works again but is worse than both members (Arsuf mean recall FF 1–25 %: 27.9 vs 35.0 / 32.2). Soups across seeds are ruled out a priori (different random heads).
- **BN re-estimation alone on M0 s0 (no averaging, "AdaBN" with new-camera training frames):** Arsuf 35.0 -> 38.8 (old camera), boson_1015 56.2 -> 54.6 and **boson_0858 91.8 -> 49.5** (deployed camera; recall 32 % at 0.3). It mostly lowers all confidences (fewer false fires at a fixed threshold, lower recall); no gain at matched false fire on the camera that matters. Not useful for deployment.

## 4. Queue v10 (when an L4 is available): SWA and ensemble distillation; criteria fixed now

- **SWA** (`src/train/swa_average.py`): continue N2 s0 and M0 s1 for 15 epochs at constant lr 0.0005 from their best.pt, average the last 10 epoch checkpoints, re-estimate BN on 1,500 training images. **Kept** if the SWA model beats its own source model on mean recall FF 1–25 % on BOTH Arsuf and boson_1015 by > 2 pts, with boson_0858 not lower by > 1 frame (paired, same seed).
- **Ensemble distillation** (`src/data/make_pseudo_labels.py`): teacher = ensemble A (M0 s1 + N2 s0 + R0 s0), run on unlabelled frames of training sessions only (Boson training sessions, 06-23 s2 < 380 s, 07-15 < 370 s; held-out / 07-02 / 07-30 excluded); fused ≥ 0.5 -> uav box, all < 0.15 -> empty, in between -> skipped. Student **KD1** = N2 data + pseudo-labels, yolo26s-p2 from rgbcurrent, seeds 0 and 1. **Adopted** over N2 if its seed mean beats N2's on Arsuf and boson_1015 by more than the larger seed spread, with no 0858 drop beyond spread; the false-fire sweep is reported alongside. The ensemble A itself (Arsuf 46.8, 1015 62.1) is the ceiling to compare with.

- **§4 rule (> 2 pts better on Arsuf AND boson_1015): both fail.** M0 s1 SWA collapses on the old-camera sets (fires on 63 % of empty Arsuf frames at 0.3, vs 27 %); N2 s0 SWA loses 5–6 points on Arsuf and 1015 and gains on 0858 only.
- Likely cause: the constant-lr continuation moves the models away from their early-stopped optimum (best epochs were 17 and 34, i.e. they had started to overfit after that), and averaging those later checkpoints keeps the drift. SWA would have to replace the late part of training (cyclic/constant lr from about the best epoch with a held-out stopping rule), not be bolted on after it.
- **Bug found and fixed while checking queue v10:** `make_pseudo_labels.py` excluded "thermal.mp4" (the 07-02 test video) by substring, which also skipped every Boson `*_thermal.mp4`; the teacher had only labelled 06-23. Fixed (exact-name match), partial pseudo-labels deleted, the labelling stage restarted at 12:20 with the Boson training sessions included (held-out sessions still excluded). KD1 starts after it.
- **Pseudo-labels after the fix (12:25):** 2,193 frames (1,173 with a drone, 1,020 empty) from 12 Boson training sessions + 06-23 s2 + 07-15. But 565 of 600 frames from 07-15 came out "empty": the drone is in nearly every frame of those flights and the teacher misses dark drones over terrain, so those empties would teach the student to miss exactly that case. **Rule tightened before KD1 trained (KD1 s0 stopped at epoch 3 and restarted):** positives all kept; empty frames kept only for Boson frames whose label status is `neg` (day and thermal detectors both silent at labelling time); old-session empties dropped. Result: 1,173 positives + 91 empties; KD1 train = 6,109 (N2) + 1,264 = 7,373 images. §4 criteria unchanged.
