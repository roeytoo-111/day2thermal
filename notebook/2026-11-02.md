## Dirty-lens augmentation (`src/data/aug_dirty_lens.py`)
- **What 13_21 shows:** dirty lens vs 13_18 (same day and terrain): a bright veil hugging the frame border with a dark centre (auto-gain), lost sharpness (median Laplacian std 3.1 vs 11.5).
- **The augmentation:**
  - rounded-rectangle border veil of +60–160 grey levels with smooth random variation;
  - auto-gain re-stretch;
  - local contrast × 0.45–0.85;
  - 1–3 smudge patches;
  - slight blur.
- **Labels:** a box is dropped when the drone falls below 12 grey levels of contrast after augmentation.
- **Check:** side by side with real 13_21 frames after two iterations (the first was far too mild; the second had too sharp an edge).
