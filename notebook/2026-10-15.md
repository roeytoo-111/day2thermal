## 5. Clouds: mine real hard negatives, don't generate

**Why not generate empty sky:** generated sky has its own look, so a model can learn "generated vs real" instead of "cloud ≠ drone".

**Source: Open Images**, images CC BY 2.0 and labels CC BY 4.0, so commercial use is allowed with attribution.
- Candidates are val+test images with a human-verified Cloud or Sky label and no positive label for anything that flies: aircraft, airliner, airplane, fixed-wing, jet, glider, helicopter, drone, bird, insect, butterfly, kite, balloon, hot-air balloon, parachute.
- That gives 15,649 images, all CC BY 2.0; downloaded (4.7 GB).
- **Catch:** "Sky" tags any photo with sky visible. On a 300-image test fw4 fired on 48%, almost all "airplane" on frame-filling cars and boats. Not our scene.
- **Filters:** the image must be sky-dominated (≥ 50% smooth thumbnail blocks) or Cloud-labelled, and the false box must be small (≤ 30% of the image side). On the test, 108/300 images count as sky, and fw4 fires on 10% of them.

The full run (`src/data/mine_open_images_negatives.py`) writes 640 tiles at native resolution into a `review_rgb_tiles.py` queue plus `attribution.csv`.

### Open Images result

**Scale of the run:** of 15,648 images, 4,232 are sky-dominated or Cloud-labelled. fw4 put a small uav/airplane box on 319 of them (7.5%) at ≥ 0.1: 236 at ≥ 0.15, 159 at ≥ 0.25. That gives 395 review tiles (344 called airplane, 51 uav).

**The high-confidence "false alarms" are mostly real flying objects Open Images did not label:** eagles and other birds, parrots, kites, a military transport aircraft, a plane by a mast. The "nothing flying" negative is incomplete, so nothing goes in unreviewed.

**Another fw4 failure mode:** flying birds called *airplane* at 0.8–0.9.

**Genuine false alarms** sit at lower confidence (0.12–0.4): wires, cloud edges and a bright gap in storm clouds, sea surface, cliffs, a wind turbine, a bridge.
