- **Capacity matters** if C_m or C_l beats C_s by more than M0's seed spread (~4 pp) on both sets. Then the larger model is the teacher candidate for distillation (teacher labels unlabelled thermal frames → student). Otherwise the bottleneck is data, and distillation is not worth it now.
