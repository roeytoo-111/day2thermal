## 5. Leak check on the labelled Arsuf bench videos + first scores on the new GT

**What the videos are.** Picture comparison (80×64 normalised frames, best match per sample):
- `th_output.ts` = **`detection/data/videos/thermal.mp4` (07-02 s1)**, from its 309 s mark to the end: 45/45 samples at distance 0.000, constant offset (bench t → file t + 309 s). No other thermal video matches (07-15 median 0.148, 07-30 0.162, 06-23 s2 0.183).
- `day_output.ts` = **`detection/data/videos/day.mp4` (07-02 s1)**, same offset (+310 s; distances 0.00–0.11, 07-15 day 0.73 median).
- So the bench is the second half of our existing test recording (the "0708" set: 320 pos / 246 neg at every 40th frame). The new GT is a dense extension of the same held-out video (3,186 thermal + 2,749 day frames), not a new unseen video, and it is the OLD thermal camera.

**Was it trained on?**
- Thermal detectors: no. thermal.mp4 was removed from training on 09-28 (the `^\d+_png` Roboflow family = the whole video, 45 % of that set); every model since (L, M0, M4, S1, C_*) descends from `thermal-1-noleak`; sessions used for training are 06-23 and 07-15; 07-02 is never a detector source (paste backgrounds, label transfer and pasted sprites exclude it). The retired leaked models (coco_baseline, rgb_transfer) did see it.
- The diffusion translator (not the detector) trained on 07-02 s1 pairs; its translated backgrounds only went into the A/B runs that used 06-23/07-15 crops.
- RGB: Fixed_wing_v3 is a Roboflow export of 2026-03-06 and its content is simulator/web/older recordings (checked 24 random images); the recording is from 2026-07-02, four months later. The production model's own dataset (Fixed_wing_v2) has the same README (2026-03-06 export, cleaned by Daniel on 07-06/07). **Not verifiable:** its starting weights `yolo26s_small_07072026` (trained after the recording) — ask what that was trained on. The 10-06 Boson addon is a different day.
- Bias to remember: day boxes were pre-filled by the production RGB tracks and thermal boxes by M0 tracks, then reviewed; this flatters those two models a little (not a training leak).
