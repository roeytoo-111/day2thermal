## 2. Thermal data: Svanström IR (CC0)

365 clips, 320×256 native (the repo's demo clip is upscaled), one labelled target per frame; 157 drone / 79 bird / 74 airplane / 55 helicopter. Labels checked visually on 10 random clips (boxes correct in native pixels). Ground camera looking up at sky over a treeline: our main case.
`src/data/svanstrom_to_yolo.py`: 1 frame/s, ×2 upscale to 640×512, drone → uav (0), bird → bird (1), airplane/helicopter → empty labels (hard negatives); split by clip 70/10/20.

| run | change vs reference | reference |

Metric: mean recall FF 1–25% on boson_0858 / boson_1015 (10_15_59 also without the 14 suspect frames, until Daniel's re-review).
- **S1 adopted** if its seed mean is ≥ M0's on both sets and above on at least one by more than the larger seed spread of the two, and 0708_sky / 0730_sky do not drop > 5 pp. The Svanström test split is reported (in-domain), not decisive. Once the Arsuf GT exists it becomes a third decisive set.
- **Capacity matters** if C_m or C_l beats C_s by more than M0's seed spread (~4 pp) on both sets. Then the larger model is the teacher candidate for distillation (teacher labels unlabelled thermal frames → student). Otherwise the bottleneck is data, and distillation is not worth it now.
- C_s vs M0 shows the effect of the drone-RGB initialisation on the new camera (single seed; read as a trend).
