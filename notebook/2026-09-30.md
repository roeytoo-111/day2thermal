## 4. Diffusion route (CtrLoRA): Zenodo record 7465521, tested

**Record:** "RGB-LWIR Labeled Dataset for Air-based platforms", Gallagher & Oughton (George Mason), **CC-BY-4.0**. Files: `car.zip`, `person.zip`, `truck.zip` (3.2 GB), extracted to `data/diffusion/zenodo/x/` (gitignored).

**What "12,600 labelled images" actually is:**
- 3 classes × 3 modalities (rgb/tir/fuse) × **100 base images** × 7 variants, plus a VOC `.xml` per image.
- The 7 variants are the original plus six image-processing augmentations: blurred, flipped, flipped+blurred, DoG, Sobel, Gaussian threshold. Only the originals are photographs.
- Numbering is per modality: RGB `101–200`, TIR `201–300`, fused `1–100`.
- **Net: 300 unique RGB/TIR scene pairs** (100 per class).

**Tests:**

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Are RGB k+100 and TIR k+200 the same scene? Edge-map NCC after a scale and translation search, vs shuffled pairs (36 sampled) | **Yes.** Matched median 0.448 vs shuffled 0.234; 97% of matched pairs score above the shuffled p90 |
| Pixel-aligned? Both resized to 320×256, then residual shift by phase correlation | **No.** Residual shift median 5.5 px (1.7%), p90 14.5 px (4.5%); image sizes differ per pair. Needs per-pair registration. |
| Is the thermal intensity or a palette? | **False-colour** (iron-like: purple→orange→yellow). Best standard colormap fit is off by a median 30/255; the first colour principal component explains 68%. **Not reliably invertible** without the exact camera palette; the stretch also varies per image. |
| Domain | Downward/oblique drone views of parking lots, fields and roads with cars/people. Almost no sky, no airborne targets, no upward-looking geometry. Far from our deployment view. |
| **SD1.5 VAE round-trip** (`sd-vae-ft-mse`, the latent space CtrLoRA's SD1.5 base works in) on 60 real eval frames at native 640×512, drone contrast before and after by GT box size | **Drones survive.** Contrast retained (median / p10): 0–6 px 90%/75%; 6–9 px 91%/78%; 9–16 px 81%/74%; 16–32 px 96%/88%; ≥32 px 99%/89%. Contrast-to-noise barely changes. **The 8× latent compression is not a blocker** (my hypothesis refuted). |

**Assessment:**
- **Usable Zenodo data:** at most ~300 pairs after per-pair registration and a palette mapping we can't do reliably, in a very different domain.
- **Plus our 280 in-domain pairs:** ~580 total. That's below CtrLoRA's ~1k-pair comfort zone, and most of it is off-domain.
- **The two remaining blockers are not architectural:**
  1. **RGB doesn't encode temperature.** The biggest recall hole (§3) is dark drones over sun-heated terrain, a *thermal* phenomenon. In RGB those drones are dark in any case. A translator can only learn "drone → dark" if its training pairs show that over terrain; Zenodo has no drones, and our pairs have only this one afternoon.
  2. **Per-frame diffusion flickers on video.** It's fine for single training images, not for "pass my RGB videos and get IR video".
- **Recommendation:**
  - Don't build CtrLoRA on this data now.
  - If the GAN go/no-go (E17) passes, the translator question is answered more cheaply. If it fails, diffusion remains the fallback. Use it only with our in-domain pairs, and gate it with the same `gan_gonogo.py` criteria (it must beat the linear colour baseline).
  - Zenodo is **CC-BY 4.0, commercially usable**, unlike MMFW/LRDD. Its real value is as thermal background/clutter data for the detector (terrain seen from above: vehicles, people, heated ground), not as translation pairs.
- **Next data step with the clearest payoff:** generator **v3 with dark-polarity drones over cluttered terrain.** It targets the 86-frame bucket at 22% recall directly.

## 9. Misalignment-tolerant training instead of CycleGAN (Daniel's question)

- **Unpaired CycleGAN discards the strongest signal we have:** which thermal pixel goes with which RGB pixel, known to within 1–4 px (<1% of the image). The earlier unpaired run on 07-08 produced output that looked like greyscale RGB (the class-0 images).
- **Better fit: paired methods that tolerate misalignment.**
  - RegGAN (Kong et al., NeurIPS 2021) learns a correction field for noisy pairs jointly with the generator.
  - A cheaper version for our pix2pix: compute L1 on blurred or downsampled output/target (shift-tolerant) and let the PatchGAN handle local detail.
- **With that, more data is usable:** frames outside verified segments (via the nearest segment's H) and denser sampling. Roughly 2× the current pairs.

## 10. Correction: labelling-tool box bug (click auto-fit capped at 51 px)

- **The bug:** the click auto-fit grew a same-contrast region inside a ±24 px window. On textured terrain the region bleeds into the clutter until it hits the window edge, and the tool silently saved a 51×51 box.
- **Affected, all from clicks:**
  - 07-08 eval set: 54 of 280 drone boxes (19%; 17 capped in both dimensions)
  - 06-23: 6 of 208
  - 07-15: 66 of 88 (75%)

  The drone is inside those boxes but not centred, and the size is meaningless. Inspected on 07-15: thin dark fixed-wing silhouettes (~15–25 px span) over terrain.
- **What it invalidates in §3:**
  - The "≥32 px bucket: 8% recall" and "dark drones median 25 px" statistics, and the dark/bright split. Polarity was computed from box contents vs a ring, and a 51 px box over terrain holds both dark and bright clutter.
  - Dark drones over terrain definitely exist (seen in overlays and in the 07-15 crops), but **the 86/97 counts and their recall must be recomputed after the redo.**
  - The located-recall totals are affected less: the hit rule is centre distance ≤ 0.75 × long side (~38 px for a capped box). But it's lenient for exactly those frames.
- **Fix** (`gt_boxes.py`):
  - **Drag-to-draw** on either panel.
  - **Polarity-aware click fit** (`fit_click`): it snaps to local contrast and returns *None* instead of a capped box when it can't isolate the target, then asks for a drag.
  - **Every fitted box is shown before saving,** with `+`/`-` to resize and Enter to accept.
  - **A mouse gesture always means "this is the target".** Before, on unlabelled frames, click-then-Enter recorded "nothing".
  - **`--redo-capped`** revisits only the capped frames.
- **Redo needed (Daniel):** 54 + 6 + 66 = 126 frames. Then re-run the located scoring and the polarity/size breakdown.
