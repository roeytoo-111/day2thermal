**Cause found (no model outputs needed): content coverage.** RGB sky fraction per pair (`diffusion/diagnose.py`):

| split / day | terrain (< 20% sky) | mixed | sky (> 50%) |
|---|---|---|---|

- **Implication 1:** any translator meant to convert our RGB videos, which contain terrain, must train on 07-15.
- **Implication 2:** day generalisation must be tested on a day whose content training covers.

## 7. Diagnosis on the actual 07-15 predictions (`diagnose.py`)

| content (n) | model L1 / SSIM / edge NCC | linear baseline L1 / SSIM / edge NCC |
|---|---|---|
| **sky > 50% (13)** | **0.133 / 0.515 / 0.465** | 0.201 / 0.400 / 0.312 |
| mixed (147) | 0.177 / 0.299 / 0.359 | 0.180 / 0.293 / 0.267 |
| terrain (132) | 0.204 / 0.186 / 0.253 | 0.203 / 0.198 / 0.179 |

1. **Not an AGC or level problem.** Mean level matches (0.421 vs 0.426), and a per-image affine fit barely helps (L1 0.190 → 0.189).
2. **Content coverage confirmed.** On sky scenes of the unseen day the model beats the baseline clearly (L1 −34%, SSIM +0.12). On terrain it's no better. The worst strips (looking down at terrain) are invented.
3. **Output far too smooth: hf_ratio 0.04** (4% of the real fine-texture energy).
   - The real thermal is strongly detail-enhanced (saturated whites, speckled vegetation), likely the camera's DDE/AGC.
   - The 7×-downscaled RGB carries none of that texture, so the model plays safe.
   - Edge placement is better than the baseline (edge NCC 0.31 vs 0.23).

**Next:**
- (a) Retrain with terrain in the data (`diff_pairs_t0623`, same config: one change).
- (b) Cheap test, no training: a fixed camera-like enhancement of the outputs (`diagnose.py --enhance clahe | unsharp | clahe+unsharp`; never fitted to targets). Does texture and SSIM move toward real?
