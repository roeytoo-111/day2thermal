## 6. Diffusion test on 07-15 → NO-GO; diagnosis

| on 07-15 (292 pairs) | L1 | SSIM | r(lum) |
|---|---|---|---|
| diffusion | 0.189 | 0.251 | +0.194 (real +0.187) |
| linear baseline | 0.191 | 0.255 | +0.021 |
| pix2pix (same test) | 0.161 | 0.303 | +0.318 |

C1 passes; C2 fails (ratio 0.99). The diffusion model only ties the per-pixel colour formula and is worse than pix2pix. No drones in the output (expected); some images look painted.

**Cause found (no model outputs needed): content coverage.** RGB sky fraction per pair (`diffusion/diagnose.py`):

| split / day | terrain (< 20% sky) | mixed | sky (> 50%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| train 07-08 / 06-23 | 0 / 0 | 38 / 22 | 329 / 274 |
| **test 07-15** | **132** | **147** | **13** |

- **The training set has no terrain-dominated pairs.** The 07-15 test is 95% terrain or mixed.
- So "unseen day" was really "unseen content." That also explains the pix2pix collapse. The painted look is the model inventing thermal terrain it never saw.
- **Implication 1:** any translator meant to convert our RGB videos, which contain terrain, must train on 07-15.
- **Implication 2:** day generalisation must be tested on a day whose content training covers.
