# 2026-09-28

## 1. Context

- **Goal:** a thermal (LWIR) drone detector that works on the deployment camera (640×512). Real labelled thermal data is scarce, so the plan was to generate synthetic thermal and/or add external data.
- **Detector baselines:** YOLO11n `coco_baseline` and `rgb_transfer`, trained on `thermal-1-filtered` (Roboflow, class `0` dropped because it is GAN output). Reported recall on `thermal.mp4` was ~78%.
- **GAN repo (`day2thermal/`):** pix2pix, ThermalGAN two-stage, and CycleGAN. Only CycleGAN had produced thermal output, and it was never evaluated. The only other trained model is a 6-epoch RGB→NIR pix2pix (not thermal).

## 3. Results

**Eval set:** `data/recall_ground_truth/manifest.csv`, every 40th frame of `thermal.mp4`, labelled blind to the models: 257 positive / 340 empty frames (16 unlabelled). A frame counts as caught if there is a detection within ±2 frames. 95% Wilson CIs.

**Correction to the table shared today:** `thermal_dets_p2_noleak.json` was produced with conf ≥ **0.05**. The two older files start at **0.10**, and 30% of P2's detections fall below 0.1. ### How to read these

- **Table A is not a model comparison.** The leaked models' numbers there are memorisation. On the frames they never saw (table B) the same models drop from 77% to 33–38% recall.
- **Table B is a fair comparison but too small to decide anything.** Every CI overlaps. We cannot currently tell these three models apart on clean data.
- **The tail is intrinsically harder.** p2_noleak, which is clean everywhere, gets 68% on the full video but 25% on the tail. Only compare numbers from the same subset.
- **p2_noleak's recall falls steeply with threshold** (68% → 37% from 0.1 to 0.5), while the leaked models barely move. Its detections are low-confidence. That is consistent either with honest uncertainty on unseen data or with an undertrained P2 branch. Check the training log: how many weights were transferred, and that 4 detection scales (P2–P5) were reported.
- **The honest baseline to beat is now p2_noleak on the full video:** 67.7% recall / 23.2% false-fire at conf ≥ 0.1.

## 4. Findings

1. **The training/eval leak was a whole session.** The `^\d+_png` family is `thermal.mp4` sampled every 5th frame (frame ≈ 5.002·index + 14, max residual 15 frames): 2260 train images, 45% of the set, covering frames 19–19,878. The 1510 "unconfirmed" images are the neighbours of the 750 confirmed ones. valid/test don't contain it.
2. **The previous ~78% recall was mostly memorisation** (table B).
3. **There is a size mismatch.** Training drones have a median of 40 px; eval-video drones are ~13 px or smaller. Option A targets exactly this.
4. **The existing "GAN thermal" is a greyscale filter.** Output correlates 0.81–0.92 with RGB luminance, drones come out dark while real thermal drones are bright, and the thermal sky gradient is absent. The class-0 images look the same way.
5. **The streams are synced:** thermal_time = day_time + 919 ms. Two independent methods agree (ego-motion over 10 windows at 915–924 ms; KLV at 910–942 ms). `day.mp4`/`thermal.mp4` are **not** the arsuf `.ts` recording; the earlier +893 ms belongs to arsuf. My first pairing claim, based on one pose match with arsuf's offset, was unfounded; it happened to be close.
6. **Registration needs a lens model and time segments.** The thermal lens has strong barrel distortion, and the day camera's pose relative to the thermal steps between stretches of time (~1.5° roll, ~14 px). Frames with near-field objects (the tent) have parallax and are excluded.
7. **Paired data is small.** After registration coverage (63%), scene-change subsampling and the train/val time split, there are 392 pairs. Diversity is one afternoon, one site, one weather.

## 5. Decisions and exit criteria

**GAN (pix2pix on the 392 pairs) is a single, time-boxed go/no-go.** No further registration work (for example lens-centre refinement) and no `two_stage` unless it passes.
- **Run:** `python -m day2thermal.train --data-root data/vid_pairs --out runs/vid_p2p --mode pix2pix --load-size 0 --crop-size 128 --num-scales-d 2`. At most ~2 GPU hours.
- **Pass requires all three:**
  1. **Not a greyscale filter.** `check_thermalness` correlation of generated vs luminance is below 0.75 *and* within 0.1 of the real thermal vs luminance correlation on the same pairs.
  2. **Beats a trivial baseline.** Fit thermal ≈ a·luminance + b on the train pairs. pix2pix must beat it clearly on val L1 and SSIM. If it can't beat a linear brightness map, it learned nothing thermal.
  3. **Qualitative.** Cold sky at the top with the right gradient, bright clouds and ground, and drones rendered **bright**, on ≥ 3 of 4 val strips.
- **If any check fails, stop.** Redirect the time to option A variants, external real IR data (LRDDv3, Anti-UAV), a second recorded session, and scoping diffusion (CtrLoRA, built for low-data paired translation).
- **Expectations, even on a pass:** a translator can't predict where drones are hot, so a pass buys realistic backgrounds (fewer false fires), not drone recall.

**Detector attribution.** p2_noleak changed two things at once (leak fix + P2). Needed:
- **`rgb_transfer_noleak`** (plain RGB weights, no P2, leak-free data). This isolates P2's effect.
- **`…_p2_synthA`** (E14, running). Compare against p2_noleak on the full video, matched conf floor, plus a threshold sweep.
