## 4. Results

### 4.1 Detector, location-aware (a hit must land on the drone box, same frame; conf ≥ 0.1)
All labels verified, frame indexing fixed (§5).

| set | positive / negative frames | p2_noleak recall / FF | p2_synthA recall / FF | synthA at p2's FF |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 07-08 all | 320 / 246 | **42.2%** / 25.2% | 63.1% / 75.6% | ~38% @ 24%: worse |
| 07-08 airborne (200 < f < 23600) | 296 / 241 | 44.6% / 25.7% | 67.6% / 75.9% | ~41%: worse |
| 07-30 (test 2) | 137 / 279 | **21.9%** / 6.1% | 29.2% / 22.9% | ~16% @ 6%: worse |
| 06-23 | 212 / 15 | 70.3% / (n=15) | 77.4% / (n=15) | FF unmeasurable |
| 07-15 (dark drones over terrain) | 86 / 121 | **1.2%** / 8.3% | 2.3% / 23.1% | both ≈ 0 |

- The old frame-level metric (any detection anywhere within ±2 frames) overstated recall: 67.7% for p2 vs 42% located.
- On 07-30, frame recall ≈ located recall, so the model mostly doesn't fire: a camera/session gap.

### 4.2 SynthA v1 (copy-paste, any placement)
- It learned "small bright point = drone".
- It fires on static terrain hot spots and clutter (4× p2's false-fire at conf 0.5, excluding moving objects).
- It is **worse than p2 at matched false-fire on every set**.
- It did help its target case (tiny bright drones in sky) and hurt dark drones.

  - Local polarity flip: 2·bg − I inside the box, background unchanged.
- **Not added:** blur (the base set is already less sharp than the deployment camera, 421 vs 1164); rotation and stripe noise (marginal). Flips and brightness jitter are Ultralytics defaults.
- **Caveat:** flipped base drones are mostly dark-on-sky, which is rare in reality.

## 5. Bugs found and fixed (all affect earlier numbers; corrected above)

| bug | effect | fix |
|---|---|---|
| Click auto-fit capped boxes at 51 px on clutter | 54/280 (07-08), 66/88 (07-15) boxes meaningless in size | Drag-to-draw, polarity-aware fit, confirm step, `--redo-capped` |
| Click then Enter saved "nothing" (unlabelled or empty-labelled frames) | Missed drones: 44 recovered on 07-08 | Mouse gesture = target; `--verify` pass with the previous label pre-filled (Enter keeps) |
| Detections 1-based vs GT 0-based | ±2 points located recall | `--det_index_base 1` in the scorer |
| cv2 seek ≠ sequential frame (VFR) | 07-30 labels pointed N−106…N+65 frames away (recall 10% → 22% after fix); 06-23 N−2 | `remap_seek_frames.py` (pixel-exact match, 539/539 and 235/235); `FrameReader` always decodes sequentially; exports verified byte-identical |

**Verify pass result:** 07-08 went from 280 to **320 drones** (246 nothing, 23 unsure, 8 no-drone-visible). Large boxes that remain are genuine (hand launch, aircraft on the ground at landing).

## 6. Findings

1. **The detector's real weaknesses:**
   - Dark drones over sun-heated terrain: ≈0% on 07-15, 22% on 07-08's dark subset.
   - Tiny targets: two thirds of eval drones are < 8 network px; training drones are 6.5× larger.
   - A new session/camera (07-30): 22%.
2. **Frame-level recall is misleading;** only location-aware scoring at matched false-fire separates models.
3. **Paired RGB→thermal translation doesn't generalise across days** with ~700 pairs (pix2pix NO-GO), and RGB can't show where a drone is hot.
4. **Label quality is decisive:** the audit and verify pass changed 07-08 by 40 drones and 07-30 by up to 106 frames of offset.
