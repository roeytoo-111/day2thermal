## 7. E18 result: `p2_noleak_synthA` (added later the same day)

**Headline as first scored:** 94.6% recall / 79.1% false-fire at conf ≥ 0.1. **Don't use this number.** Decomposed:

**(a) The recall metric doesn't check location.** A positive frame counts as caught if there's *any* detection within ±2 frames. SynthA averages 2.96 detections per frame (p2_noleak: 0.69) and fires on 79% of empty frames, so much of that recall is chance.

**(b) Threshold sweep** (613 GT frames, matched floors):

| conf ≥ | p2_noleak recall / FF | synthA recall / FF |
|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | 67.7% / 23.2% | 94.6% / 79.1% |
| 0.3 | 48.6% / 9.1% | 84.0% / 48.2% |
| 0.5 | 37.0% / 4.4% | 69.6% / 29.4% |
| 0.6 | 26.5% / 2.9% | 53.7% / 17.1% |
| 0.7 | 12.1% / 1.5% | 25.3% / 3.2% |

At a **matched false-fire rate** synthA is no better, probably worse. At ~23% FF: p2 67.7% vs synthA ~62%. At ~3–4% FF: p2 37% vs synthA ~25%. And even that comparison is inflated by (a).

**(c) What the false fires are** (empty frames, conf ≥ 0.3, frames containing each hit type):

| | p2_noleak | synthA |
|---|---|---|
| Moving small object (tracked across ±10 frames) | 5 | 61 |
| Static small hot spot | 3 | 53 |
| Large clutter (vegetation, tent, glare, clouds) | 23 | 59 |
| Isolated | 1 | 13 |
| FF excluding moving-object-only frames | 7.9% | 34.4% |

**(d) The GT has missed tiny objects.** Frames 3120–3720 are all labelled empty, but a 1–2 px object drifts across frames 3200–3600 in the thermal. Mapped through the registration into the 4K day frame (frame 3280, segment 2), there is a small dark airborne object ~3 thermal px from the predicted point. It's real, either the drone far away or a bird. So some of synthA's "false fires" are true small-object detections, and the "empty" labels are unreliable for 1–3 px targets.

**Verdict:** option A v1 is **not a win**. It taught "small bright point = drone":
- More sensitivity to real tiny moving objects (good).
- Many more hits on static terrain hot spots and clutter (bad); still ~4× p2's FF at conf 0.5 after excluding moving objects.
- Likely causes: pastes land on terrain as well as sky (background level up to 200); a third are generic parametric blobs; synthetic images are 50% of the training set.

## 8. Next steps (revised)

1. **Option A v2, a generator fix; compare to p2_noleak only:**
   - Paste only in sky (low-texture, low-gradient regions). Drones in this footage are airborne, and unlabelled terrain hot spots then act as implicit negatives.
   - Drop parametric blobs, keeping real downscaled crops only.
   - Cut the synthetic share from 50% to ~20–25%, with 1–2 pastes per image.
2. **Targeted GT audit, not a relabel:** review only the ~60–80 empty-labelled frames where either model fires on a tracked moving small object, using the RGB crop when it's in the field of view. Mark drone / bird / nothing.
3. **Location-aware recall.** Recall must check that the hit is on the drone. This needs one box per positive frame (257 frames). It could be semi-automatic: propose the best tracked detection, a human confirms.
4. GAN go/no-go (E17) is unchanged.
