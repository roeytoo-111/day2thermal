### Near-duplicate detection: three failed versions before one that works

1. **dHash alone (Hamming ≤ 4) chained 14k of 24.8k v3 images into one group.** Any two skies with the same brightness gradient get the same hash. Unrelated-source pairs were confirmed by eye.
2. **Adding high-pass thumbnail correlation (NCC ≥ 0.9, detail std ≥ 6) still left a 4,381-image group.** Roboflow's rotation augmentation leaves black corners, and their edges match between any two images rotated by the same angle.
3. **Final: fingerprint the central 60% of the image only.** The largest group is now one 1,202-image screenshot series. BirdsFlying, Bird_JPEG and birds_flying merge, which is correct: same footage.

Name rule: generic names (`img_0029`, `image_3`, ...) are not a series. v3 has 7,120 `img_*` files from several unrelated subsets.

### v3 audit (`results/rgb_audit/v3/AUDIT.md`)

- **Leakage:** 58.6% of valid and 56.8% of test images share a source with train. 13.8% / 12.2% have a near-duplicate picture in train.
- **Small drones:** uav < 16 px (model-input px at 640) are 5.2% of train boxes but 0.7% / 0.7% of valid / test.
- **Small, low-contrast drones on a textured background** (cloud, terrain): 40 boxes in train, 5 in valid, 1 in test.
- **Airplane:** 1,226 train boxes, 55% of them ≥ 128 px. Distant aircraft (< 32 px) are about 140 boxes.
- **Negatives and lighting:** empty images are 7.9% of valid and 6.6% of test. Dark scenes are about 5%.
- **Partial v4 train:** < 16 px uav 8.5%, small + low contrast + textured only 23 boxes.
