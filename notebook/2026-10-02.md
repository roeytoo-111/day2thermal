## 6. Seed noise (run S) is as large as most effects measured so far: decision rule changed

**S = pasteB with seed 1, nothing else changed.** At FF ≤ 10% on the old grid: 0730_sky 52 → 64 (+12), 0708_sky 65.7 → 61.4; at FF ≤ 5%, ±14–16 pp. Two sources of noise:
1. **Training:** seed, and the epoch where early stopping lands.
2. **Scoring:** 13 coarse thresholds, and FF ≤ 5% = 12 of 246 negative frames.

**Scorer change (`score_models.py`):**
- Every distinct score ≥ the floor is now a candidate threshold, so recall @FF ≤ x is exact.
- New column **"mean recall FF 1–25%"**: exact recall averaged over FF budgets 1%..25%, one smoother number per model.
- Tables: `results/scores_2026-10-02_exact.md`.

**Seed spread, |B − S| on mean recall FF 1–25%:** 0708_all 1.2, 0708_sky 2.1, 0730_test2 7.3, **0730_sky 11.4**, 0715 0.1.
- **0708 is stable across seeds; 0730 is not.** 0730 is another camera session, and the seed decides how well a model generalizes to it; its sky subset is only 75 drones.

**Re-reading G and H with this metric:**
